Scoreboard Definitions

Precise definitions reduce disputes and align with event tracking. Each scoreboard maps 1:1 to instrumented events.

Qualified Trial

Prospect books an intro on your calendar, confirms via SMS, and replies “Y” (or equivalent) to qualify.

Event: qtrial_booked

Show

The booked intro logs attendance in your system or GAJO’s check-in form.

Event: show_attended

New Member

First dues paid (captured via Stripe/CRM webhook or manual confirmation).

Event: member_activated

All events are tracked in GA4/GTM and your dashboard. You can audit the data anytime.
